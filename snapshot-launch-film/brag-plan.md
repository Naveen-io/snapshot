# Brag Plan: SNAPSHOT

## What is this app?
Snapshot is a precision screenshot, annotation, and visual composition workspace for Windows that transforms raw screen captures into App Store showcase presentations in seconds.

## The angle
The Apple product launch treatment: confident, restrained, and obsessed with craft. A raw, vulnerable app screenshot sits honestly in a pure white studio, then gets elevated into a high-precision titanium device frame surrounded by rich, calibrated studio lighting and designer backdrops. No generic SaaS buzzwords—just pure visual transformation.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A pristine, blindingly clean white studio frame (#FFFFFF). Modern editorial typography reveals:
"Your app." [deliberate, confident pause]
"Make it look the part."
Typography scales in with subtle tracking and silky opacity.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Shot 02 (3–6s) — The Honest Starting Point**: A clean raw app screenshot glides into the frame, sitting honestly on pure white with subtle ground elevation shadow.
- **Shot 03 (6–11s) — The Hero Transformation**: The raw capture elevates; a precision smartphone chassis (titanium rim, Dynamic Island, subtle glass specular gleam) wraps seamlessly around it. A warm 'Velvet Sunset' gradient backdrop blooms into existence with multi-layered diffuse drop shadows. Copy: "Framed to perfection."
- **Shot 04 (11–15s) — The Taste System**: Rhythmic, buttery-smooth transition cycling through genuine Snapshot presets:
  1. *Velvet Sunset* (warm ambient orange/pink/violet gradient)
  2. *Obsidian Luxe* (deep charcoal, subtle studio rim light)
  3. *Clean Editorial* (high-fashion minimalist monochrome)
  Micro-tags float alongside each style.
- **Shot 05 (15–18s) — The Export / Ready for the World**: The complete showcase card settles with a gentle 3D perspective glide. A floating frosted glass pill pops in: "Exported • Lossless 4K PNG".

## Outro / punchline
- **Shot 06 (18–20s) — The Resolution**: Seamless dissolve to pure white studio. The Snapshot icon settles beside the tracked-out wordmark "SNAPSHOT", with the final confident signature: "Your app. In its best light."

## User flow worth showing
Raw Screen Capture → Instant Device Framing & Depth → Studio Preset Curation → Lossless 4K PNG Export.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Apple product launch film — minimalist industrial design, restrained editorial typography, white studio photography, physically believable motion.
- Interpretation: Generous negative space, calm deliberate pacing, silky smooth easing, zero hyperactive flashing. Every line settles long enough to be read effortlessly.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920 (9:16 Reels & Shorts)
## Duration: 20 seconds (600 frames @ 30fps)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Studio Background: `#FFFFFF` (with soft `#F5F5F7` studio floor transitions)
- Text Primary: `#1D1D1F` (Apple near-black, high WCAG contrast on white)
- Text Secondary: `#86868B` (Apple neutral gray)
- Interface Accents: `#0071E3` (Apple blue) / `#10B981` (Emerald)
- Display Font: `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", sans-serif`
- Preset Palettes:
  - Velvet Sunset: `linear-gradient(135deg, #f97316, #ec4899, #8b5cf6)`
  - Obsidian Luxe: `linear-gradient(135deg, #090d16, #111827, #1f2937)`
  - Clean Editorial: `linear-gradient(135deg, #f3f4f6, #e5e7eb, #d1d5db)`

## Share copy (draft)
Stop posting raw screenshots. Snapshot frames your app in its best light.

## Audio direction
- Role: Warm, refined electronic bed with restrained, tactile UI SFX.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: Starts at 0.0s, steady 0.28 volume, subtle dip during final logo settle at 18.0s.
- Music cue guidance:
  - Strong beats: 8.74s (hero transformation climax), 13.11s (preset cycle), 17.47s (export pill pop), 18.56s (brand lockup reveal).
- Audio-reactive treatment: Subtle; soft background ambient elevation and specular gleam breathe with the bed.
- SFX posture: Sparse, motion-matched, tactile.
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - 6.2s: Soft chassis snap (`interface/drop_001.ogg`) as device frame locks into place.
  - 11.2s & 13.1s: Subtle switch clicks (`ui/switch1.ogg`) as presets transition.
  - 16.5s: Crisp confirmation chime (`interface/click_001.ogg`) on export badge reveal.
  - 18.2s: Deep resonant logo settle (`impact/impactBell_heavy_000.ogg` at gentle volume).
- Restraint rule: No cartoon whooshes, no robotic typing rattle, no harsh buzzers.
